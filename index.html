02 / About

Curiosity, applied with care.

“The form of the data changes. The aim stays the same: make the evidence useful.”

I hold an MSc in Data Science from the University of Aberdeen and a BSc (Hons) in Mathematics from MARA University of Technology in Malaysia, both with First Class Honours.

My experience spans industrial emissions analytics, live production platforms, safety analytics, NLP and LLM workflows. As long as there is a problem worth understanding and data in any form to learn from, I’m comfortable working across industries.

I enjoy collaborating across disciplines—with engineers, analysts, developers and subject-matter experts—to turn imperfect information into useful evidence and practical decisions.
