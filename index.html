04 / Generative AISelected work

AI-powered Safety Analytics

Designed a reusable Azure OpenAI GPT-4 pipeline, alongside BERT and topic-modelling methods, to turn large volumes of unstructured safety records into structured, decision-ready insight for industrial clients

Use Cases:

1. Trained BERT multi-label classifiers to predict six consequence types from free-text hazard observations (>90% accuracy for most), and used GPT-4 to infer root causes of injury cases. Enabled the client to flag high-risk observations and target proactive interventions.

2. Used GPT-4 and iterative prompt engineering to analyse incident, near-miss and audit records across 27 sites against 45 group standards, assessing whether leadership actions or omissions contributed to events (95% accuracy). Reduced manual investigation effort and surfaced risk-reduction opportunities across sites.

3. Combined LDA topic modelling with GPT-4 theme generation and manual refinement to find recurring themes and patterns, and build a standardised gap taxonomy, then re-classified all gap entries consistently (about 80% accuracy). Gave the client cross-region and site-level visibility of recurring gaps to guide targeted process improvements.
